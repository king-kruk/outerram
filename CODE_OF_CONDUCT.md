# Code of Conduct

OuterRAM is intended to be a constructive technical community. Contributors, maintainers and users participating in project spaces are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code and evidence;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and unwanted sexual attention;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information without permission;
- avoid intentionally disruptive behavior, spam or abuse of project infrastructure;
- follow the project's security disclosure process instead of publicly exploiting or disclosing an unpatched vulnerability.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues or other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate and focused on restoring a safe, productive environment.

## Reporting conduct concerns

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull-request discussion. For a sensitive conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately rather than posting personal details publicly. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to project-controlled spaces and to public representation of the project where behavior materially affects the safety of project participants.
